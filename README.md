## In 30 seconds

- **Grok is the engine.** It is shared by everyone and stays unchanged. Nothing here alters Grok or its model.
- **Jarvis (@grok) is Tommy's own layer on top.** His rules, his final veto, his record and his morning anchor message. The engine suggests. Tommy decides.
- **XiCore is the human veto core.** The rule at the centre: a model can propose, but nothing is kept, posted or acted on without a human yes. A veto is final.
- **GrokOmega Prime is the memory and refinement layer.** Raw ideas (the "stone") are refined, and only what is approved is kept as a lasting, dated record (the "gold").
- **Anyone can build their own.** Their own name, their own rules, their own veto. [STARTER.md](STARTER.md) shows how in one afternoon, for free.

**We document today so that tomorrow remembers.**

## Run the Intent Ledger (2 commands)

Needs Python 3.9 or newer. Nothing to install.

```bash
cd intent-ledger
python3 example.py              # one-minute walk through
python3 -m unittest -v          # run the tests
```
