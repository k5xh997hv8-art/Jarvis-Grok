# Update: the veto loop ran live (12 Oct 2026)


On 12 October 2026 the XiCore loop ran end to end on real items for the first time:

- Jarvis (@grok) made proposals in the evening check-in threads.
- The proposals went into a private pending list. Nothing was approved by default.
- Each one went through a rules check: the veto is absolute, only my yes writes, nothing is deleted, there are no private details, and the wording is honest.
- I gave each one my own explicit yes. Only then was it signed with my key into my private record.

Result: two signed entries, and the chain verifies. The record itself stays private, so this update covers the process, not the contents.

Two entries show the loop works. They don't show that it is finished. Grok is the engine and Jarvis is mine. The final say stays with me.
